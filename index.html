Privacy

How Alkhwarizmi AI Academy uses the details you send us.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: your name and WhatsApp number, and where a form asks, your role, organisation, email, city or a short message. We also note which page the form was sent from and, if you came from one of our links, which one.

Why we use it

To reply to you, arrange a call, a visit or a seat, and send you the dates you asked about. Nothing else.

Who sees it

The academy team, and the service that delivers website forms to our inbox. We never sell your details or share them with advertisers.

How long we keep it

While we are in touch about a programme, and no longer than two years after our last message unless you join a cohort.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time. Write to info@alkhawarizmi.ai or message us on WhatsApp.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies. It remembers your language choice on your own device.
