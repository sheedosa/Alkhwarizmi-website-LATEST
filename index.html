The course

Five evenings. Ten hours.

Delivered in Arabic, two hours each evening, Sunday to Thursday. No background needed: a phone is enough.

  1. Evening 01 / 05

    What AI is, and why it matters

    Patterns, not thinking. What it can and cannot do.

    2 labs
  2. Evening 02 / 05

    AI in your day

    Write, rewrite, summarise and translate, with prompts that work.

    3 labs
  3. Evening 03 / 05

    Protect yourself

    Deepfakes, the four scam patterns, and what never to type into AI.

    3 labs
  4. Evening 04 / 05

    AI at work

    Automate one real task from your own job. Verify before you trust.

    2 labs
  5. Evening 05 / 05

    Libya's AI future

    Jobs, Arabic AI, the rules we need. Then a Teach-Back and certificates.

    2 labs
